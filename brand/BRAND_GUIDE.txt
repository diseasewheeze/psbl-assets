LEAGUE BRAND GUIDE: 8-BIT ARCADE
================================
The league's name, short name and founding year live in brand/identity.json;
every generator reads them from there, so a rename is one edit plus a rebuild.
Every league graphic is pixel art: drawn on a small grid of square pixels and
enlarged by whole numbers with hard edges. The files in brand/kit are generated
by tools/build_brand_package.py and tools/build_discord_assets.py (drawing code
in tools/pixel_kit.py).

PALETTE
-------
The brand reads as navy, red and white.
  Navy        #0C2340   main ground (banners, headers), letter fill on light grounds
  Deep navy   #071528   deepest ground, outer keylines, counters
  Red         #BD3039   the accent: keylines, rails, rules, bars; red status stamps
  White       #FFFFFF   letter fill of the short name, the ball, titles on navy
  Off-white   #F6F7F9   textured paper ground
  Status green #3FA34D  stamps only (approved, vote passed, paid in full)

  Tones (one step lighter or darker, for grain, dither and bevels only):
  light red #D9545C (small red text on navy), dark red #8E1F27, light navy
  #16325A, sky #8FA6C7 (pending, amended and champions stamps), bleachers
  #081A30, muted text #A0AEC4, steel #9AA1A9 / #6E747C.
  Ballpark scenes: mown outfield #0F2C50 / #143660, infield clay #8C3436 with
  #72282C and #A04242 for texture.

TYPE RAMP
---------
Fonts (OFL, in brand/fonts with OFL.txt):
  Jersey 10   display: the short name, titles, stamp words. Pixel = 75 units; caps are 10 pixels tall.
  Silkscreen  labels: eyebrows, sign-off lines, the league name, EST. year.
              Caps are 5 pixels tall; Bold for the lockup line.
  Pixelify Sans  text: the league site, the Constitution (web, PDF and Google Doc) and
              other long documents. Set it with generous line spacing.

Render pixel fonts at one export pixel per font pixel (or a whole multiple),
with antialiasing off. Sizes below are export pixels per font pixel:
  Wordmark (short name)  6x the lockup unit (36 on 1920 banners, 42 on the 2000 px wordmark)
  Lockup line + EST       1x the lockup unit (6 on 1920 banners)
  Header title            8 (caps 80 px); 6 or 4 for long titles
  Header eyebrow          4 (Silkscreen, light red)
  Header sign-off         4 (Silkscreen, muted)
  Stamp word              4 to 10, as large as fits; stamp sub-line 3 or 4
A longer league name steps the lockup down until it fits (fit_lockup in pixel_kit).

GRID
----
  8 px grid: banners, channel headers (200 x 67 pixels for 1600 x 533), dividers and
  stamps. Each header pixel is 2 px wide when Discord shows it at 400 px.
  Avatars and icons: the small 14 x 14 ball on a 20 x 20 (avatars) or 16 x 16 (emoji,
  favicon) grid, so they stay sharp at Discord's 16 to 48 px.
  Official seal: 256 x 256 grid at 4 px (1024) or 1 px (256).
  Thin rule: 4 px grid, so its three lines keep their weights.

THE EMBLEM
----------
  A pixel baseball: white ball with a chalk-shaded lower right, two red seams that curve
  toward the middle, stitched with chevrons, then a red keyline and a navy keyline.
  ball_sprite() in pixel_kit draws it at any size (32 for logos and icons, 48 for headers,
  100 for the seal); the small 14 x 14 ball is drawn by hand for 64 px and below.
  It is an original mark: never replace it with a letter, a team mark or a team logo.

THE DRAG TRACTOR
----------------
  The league's mascot machine: a 56 x 28 pixel grounds-crew utility cart that drives to the
  right and drags a steel mat behind it, smoothing the infield clay. The short name is painted
  on its white body in 3 x 5 letters (up to six letters fit). Two frames (wheels straight, then
  the body bobbed up 1 pixel) for animation. Used on the title screen (invite splash), the
  league site's field, the print cover and the video outro.

BALLPARK SCENES
---------------
  Banners: navy with red rails top and bottom, bleachers (navy seats, a few red and white
  fans) fading into navy, a dithered fade to deep navy. Title screen: a padded navy outfield
  wall with a red rail, the outfield mown in diagonal navy stripes, then the infield clay with
  the drag tractor. No real ballpark landmarks.

SOUND
-----
  The soundboard (brand/sounds) is 8-bit chiptune to match: square and pulse waves, a
  triangle bass and a noise channel, loudness-matched at -14 LUFS, each under 5.2 s.

LOGOS
-----
  Wordmark lockup: the short name (white letters, red then navy keyline), then the league
  name, then EST. year between two red rules.
  On light grounds: navy letters with a white (or off-white) keyline then red.
  Emblem: the pixel baseball (white, red, navy).

  Clear space: keep empty space of at least one quarter of the letter height on every side
  of the lockup, and one quarter of the ball's width around the ball.
  Minimum size: lockup 192 px wide (lockup unit 1); emblem 16 px (small ball).

DO
--
  Enlarge only by whole numbers with nearest-neighbor scaling.
  Keep the keyline order: red then navy on white letters.
  Use the full lockup (short name over the league name over EST. year) where a full lockup is needed.
  Use status colors by meaning: green = approved/passed/paid, red = denied/failed/owed/urgent,
  sky = pending/amended/champions, white = official/final/rulings.
  Bump the ?v= version (tools/discohook_format.py ASSET_VERSION) whenever hosted art changes.

DON'T
-----
  Don't smooth, blur, anti-alias, rotate or stretch pixel art (the seal ring text is the
  only rotated text, re-thresholded to solid pixels).
  Don't use any MLB or club logo, wordmark, mascot, letter mark, uniform lettering or
  ballpark landmark. Colors are the league's own; marks are not borrowed.
  Don't put team colors on league marks, banners, headers, dividers or stamps. Team
  colors appear only on small jersey or stripe elements (a team chip, a jersey stripe).
  Don't recolour the emblem or the short name, or swap in non-pixel fonts.
  Don't mix the small 14 x 14 ball and the large ball in one graphic.
  Don't use status green anywhere except stamps (and the money and chart emoji).
